Compound Movements

Exercises that work multiple joints and muscle groups simultaneously. Examples: squat, bench press, deadlift, and pull-up. They produce the strongest hormonal response (testosterone, GH), anchor any serious program, and should be placed early in the session while energy is high.

Isolation Movements

Exercises that target a single muscle group through one joint. Examples: biceps curl, leg extension, and lateral raise. They allow precise targeting of lagging muscles, improve mind-muscle connection, and are ideal finishers after compound movements to maximize volume.

Resti
Rest — The recovery time between sets. Compound movements require longer rest (3-5 min) for neural recovery. Isolation exercises need shorter rest (45-90 sec) for metabolic stress and pump.
Tempoi
Tempo — Written as four numbers: eccentric, pause, concentric, top. Example: 3-0-1-0 means 3 s lowering, no pause, 1 s lifting, no pause at the top. “X” means lift explosively.
RIRi
RIR — Reps in reserve: how many more reps you could do before failure. RIR 1-2 means stopping 1-2 reps short of failure. RIR 0 is training to true failure.
